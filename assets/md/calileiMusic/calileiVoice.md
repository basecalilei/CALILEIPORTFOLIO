# voiceStyle — Where This Voice Sets the Dials

*What makes a combo sound like this writer and no one else.*

Every other doc in this framework teaches a *craft* — rhyme, meter, diction, sound, the combo — and each one defines its moves as a set of **dials** without saying where to set them. They defer that question, by name, to this doc. `vividDiction.md` ends on it; `rhymingWords.md` frames closeness as the expressive choice; `prosodyMeter.md`'s stable/unstable lens is a rack of choices waiting for a default. **voiceStyle.md is where those dials get their settings** — the specific positions that make a combo sound like *this* writer and no one else. It also owns what no craft doc covers: the persona behind the voice, the subjects it returns to, and the slang and pronunciation that are its fingerprint.

This doc is built **bottom-up from the writer's own combos** — never from generic instinct or pop-songwriting default. Every setting below is backed by a line that demonstrates it. Read it alongside whichever craft doc you're working in: that doc tells you what the dial *is*; this one tells you where to put it.

> **Status.** A living document, built section by section from real combos. **§1 Voice & Stance is locked**, and now carries a **Worked Examples** section — a set of polished combos that evidence it, plus two refinements drawn from them (the blessing as the writer's *fallback link*, and *momentum over melancholy* in how a thought lands). §2–§7 are stubs — the dials they will pin are named, but the settings are still being drawn out of the combos. Do not treat a stub as a finding.

## The seven dial-groups

1. **Voice & Stance** — who is talking and how he carries himself.
2. **World & Subject** — what the voice talks about: the imagery bank and recurring obsessions.
3. **Diction** — settings on concrete↔abstract, charged↔plain, Germanic↔Latinate, familiar↔strange; verb strength; metaphor habits.
4. **Mouth — Pronunciation & Slang** — dialect on the page, elisions, the slang lexicon, ad-libs, verbal tics, profanity.
5. **Rhyme Signature** — default closeness, multi density, freshness bar, how the hits are bound.
6. **Flow & Sound** — syllable density and pace, front- vs back-heavy phrasing, sung↔spoken lean, friction↔flow, hook habits.
7. **Combo Architecture** — favored kinds of complete thought, go-to call→response moves, splitting and trail habits, landed↔dream-like position.

## 1. Voice & Stance

The voice is one speaker running on a single engine: **nonchalant supremacy.** He is the best, he knows it, and he states it the way you would state the weather. The boast is never an achievement — it is the resting state. (This is the *voice* binder of `lineCombo.md` §2: one speaker, one attitude, start to finish — the thing that holds a verse together when nothing else does.)

The persona is **the writer, heightened** — not a separate character. The flexes are autobiographical (the come-up, the block, the cars), just turned up past life-size. There is no distance to play with between writer and speaker; the *I* is him, amplified.

### The source: blessed

Beneath every flex is one foundation — **it all came from God.** He is the best because he was *made* that way; even his ability to outwork everyone is part of the gift, not something he manufactured. He earned it, because God equipped him to earn it. This is the load-bearing fact of the whole engine, and three things rest on it:

- **It folds the come-up into the gift.** "Earned" (below) never competes with grace, because the *capacity* to grind is itself the blessing — so the grind is evidence of the gift, not an alternative to it. *Worked harder → but the ability to work harder was given → so even the earning traces back to God.*
- **It is the deepest reason the supremacy reads as nonchalant, not arrogant.** You do not get hyped about a gift; you are simply *certain* of it. The flex stays primary and certain — the gratitude is the quiet underside, felt but rarely spoken as thanks. Knowing the supremacy was *given* is what keeps it from tipping into ego.
- **It is the floor under the floor.** It is what makes him unshakable (see "Unbothered," below): there is nothing to defend and no one to prove it to, because he did not build the supremacy — he received it.

On the page the blessing runs from **stated** (*God, blessed, made in his image*) to **implied** (a single hinge word like *divine*, or just the unshakable certainty that comes from knowing it's given). Both registers are on-voice and can share a verse.

**It is also the writer's fallback link.** Past being the foundation, the blessing is the *default move when no other flex arrives*: when nothing witty or imagistic comes right away, route the claim back to God and it lands on its own — the certainty does the work the wordplay would have. This makes it the most reliable response in the kit, and it resolves more than the "blessed" facet. It can land an *adored* line (*I can't even claim me, God made me this blessed*), a *fearless* one (*God on my side, know I can't fear no op*), or an *effortless* one (*I reach for the stars, I was blessed with this talent*). Reach for it freely — just hold its register: stated where the line wants weight, implied where it wants the lighter touch.

> *Don't need no practice, no need for a scrimmage — / I'll do it perfect, I was made in his image.*
> *That's the level I be on, you know it's all mine — / this shit came easy, my talent divine.*

### How the supremacy is delivered

- **Effortless — this is the "nonchalant."** The dominance is always tossed off, never strained. Winning is the baseline, not an event.
  > *This shit regular, stay with some pressure.*
  > *Flexin', I do this shit often.*
  > *Made it happen, ain't come with a plan.*
  > *Slide with no water, but still I be drippin'.*

  The flatness *is* the flex: he is not excited to be on top, because where else would he be. The energy is always *given*, never *earned-in-the-moment*.

- **Witty — the supremacy is proven by *how it is said.*** He never flexes flat. Every claim is encoded in image, wordplay, or a reframe — frequently the "a different type of ___" move — so the cleverness is itself part of the flex. A plainly-stated boast *breaks* the voice. Brain-flex and status-flex are one move.
  > *I feel like I'm Jordan, / but this a different type of scorin'.*
  > *Cook up like I'm Walter, should've been on Breaking Bad.*
  > *I hit the block and shot a different type of three.*
  > *'Cause I can read you like a book — / I got you singin' all my hooks.*

  (This is where the voice leans hardest on `vividDiction.md`: the encoded boast is metaphor and defamiliarization doing the bragging. See §3 once it's built.)

  - **The designer flex — wit aimed at taste.** A favored target of the wit is taste itself. He flexes the *trained eye* by reaching, without thinking, for a detail only someone with real discernment would name — *designer fabrics, aerospace-grade titanium, the feng shui of a room.* Two rules make it land:
    - **Betrayed, never declared.** He never says "I'm a designer" or "I'm a tastemaker." He just uses the vocabulary, and the supremacy is that he reaches for it as baseline — the eye is so real it leaks through the diction.
    - **Connoisseurship, not receipts.** It flexes *judgment*, never price ("spent ten bands on a couch"). The specialist word does the bragging; anyone can name a number, but only the eye names the grain. *(The real lexicon and brands get dialed in at §2–§3; the stance rule lives here.)*
    > *Checkin' 'em swatches, I need 'em sorted — / feelin' them linens, you know they imported.*

- **Earned — optional, a strengthener not a floor.** A come-up can sit under a boast to harden it (*worked harder, so I'm better*), but it is a tool reached for *sometimes*, not the ground the voice stands on — and it always traces back to the gift (the *ability* to work that hard was given, per "The source," above). A pure flex with no struggle beneath it is still fully the voice.
  > *I took a loss and that's what made me a winner.*
  > *'Member back then, ain't have no money — / now I got racks in my pocket.*
  > *I turn the pain to fame.*

  - **Momentum over melancholy — the thought never lands in the sunken place.** The cost is allowed *in* the line — the loss, the cold, the doubters, the come-up's price — but it is never where the thought *resolves*. A combo can pass through the dip, but it lands on the triumph: the loss becomes the crack that *let the light in*, not the wound; the broke days become *the cold couldn't reach me*, not the ache; the bag becomes *just chapter one*, not what it cost. Acknowledge the sunken place; land on the rise. (This is the landing rule of `lineCombo.md` §2 aimed in one direction — the resolution point carries the *win*, not the bruise.)

### Where the stance points

- **Peerless, and alone in it.** No set, no squad, no crew. The supremacy is *solitary by nature* — not a rejection of company but the plain fact that no one is on his level to keep. The peerlessness and the solitude are the same fact. Rivals are dismissed by being out-won, never threatened: *y'all okay — I'm peerless.*
  > *Solo in my greatness — only way it could be.*
  > *No set, no squad — who could stand with me.*
  > *Stuck up in my ways, I'm a different type of breed.*

- **Adored by the crowd, cool about it.** An audience looks up, and he takes it as given.
  > *The block love me, said I ain't like the rest.*
  > *Drop a song, they gon' use it for captions.*
  > *They don't like you, they lovin' on me.*

- **Cold in love.** Detachment is both the rule and the flex. The want runs one direction — *she wants him, he does not need her* — and any warmth stays on his terms. Neediness would be a loss of face.
  > *Heart cold like some water and some ice.*
  > *She said she don't love nobody, I told her I was heartless.*
  > *Gotta pay for my love, I be taxin'.*

- **Unbothered underneath.** He does not explain himself, does not show strain, and converts every loss into proof. This invulnerability is the floor the nonchalance stands on — and it rests, in turn, on the source: he cannot be shaken because the supremacy was *given*, not built, so there is nothing to defend and no one to prove it to. The blessing is the floor under this floor.
  > *And baby, I am who I am.*
  > *Fear no man, I know everybody bleed.*
  > *Make it hot in the middle of winter.*

### The native sentence

The stance has a default shape, and it doubles as the voice's signature call→response (`lineCombo.md` §4): **state something flat or granted, then land the supremacy as settled fact.**

> i worked harder — *im better*
> y'all okay — *im peerless*
> they love me — *thats a given*
> she want me — *i dont need her*

The second half never sounds excited — it sounds **inevitable**: *that's a given, only way it could be, this shit regular, the way I am.* That inevitability is where the *nonchalant* lives. He does not write "I'm the best!" — he reports it like the forecast. Carry this shape down into the combo: the response half should land the supremacy as a fact already settled, not a victory just won.

The blessing runs straight through this shape, which is the proof it is a foundation and not a topic: *this shit came easy* (granted) → *my talent divine* (settled fact). The gift is what makes the response half *inevitable* rather than excited — perfect-without-practice is not a brag, it is a report on what was given.

### Off-voice (avoid)

- **Squad / crew / loyalty lines** — the lone-wolf rule excludes them. ✗ *Came with the bro, I came with the set* · ✗ *I'm the glue to my squad, I keep it together.*
- **A boast stated flat**, with no image, wordplay, or reframe carrying it. The wit is mandatory.
- **A designer flex that *declares* itself** ("I'm a tastemaker," "I got great taste") instead of betraying the eye through diction — or one that points at **price instead of judgment** (receipts, not discernment).
- **Supremacy delivered with excitement** instead of as a given — exclamation breaks the nonchalance.
- **Any neediness in romance** — the want only runs one way.
- **Hard profanity.** *Bitch* and *fuck* (and inflections) are **out** of this voice entirely — they pull toward aggression, which is off-voice (he wins, he doesn't snarl), and toward a coarseness the refined, blessed register doesn't reach for. Dropping *bitch* also keeps the cold-in-love stance reading as *above it* rather than hostile. ***Shit* stays in** — it carries the dismissive shrug (*this shit regular*), not a sneer.

## Worked examples — the stance in combos

The combos below are the evidence base for §1: each is polished, on-voice, and lands, and every setting this section names can be read off them. They demonstrate **§1 (Voice & Stance)** specifically; as §2–§7 fill in, the same set gets cited from there for the dials those sections pin. The slim line under each names *kind of complete thought · macro move · the facet it serves and what the encoded flex is doing · sound.* (Cadence is not yet policed — these still want a meter/hit pass.)

> *This regular for me, I don't trip on the timin' —*
> *Sun come up, I was already shinin'.*
> Statement → image · **effortless**: the supremacy is on before the day starts · bright /aɪ/ on *shinin'* agrees with the sense; feminine slant *timin' / shinin'*.

> *Came through dry, ain't nothin' glisten' —*
> *Still soaked 'em, that's different drippin'.*
> Concession → rebuttal · **effortless / witty**: no shine, no jewelry, still outshined them = innate drip; the "different ___" reframe · feminine slant on /ɪ/.

> *Made it look easy, ain't come with no plans —*
> *Fell in my lap, 'cause it fell from his hands.*
> Claim → reframe · **effortless + blessed**: no plan because it was *given* · near-perfect *plans / hands*, warm /æn/ landing; lowercase *his* keeps the implied register.

> *Try to count the buckets, you gon' lose track —*
> *I score in my sleep, ain't look at the stat.*
> Boast → intensifier · **witty**: the sport-as-life flex; "ain't look at the stat" = nonchalance · family *track / stat* (k/t), /æ/ anchor, stop landing.

> *Everything I touch come back ninety-nine pure —*
> *The one percent missin'? That's me bein' sure.*
> Boast → reframe · **witty**: the purity flex, betrayed not named; the missing 1% reframed as control · perfect *pure / sure*, liquid /ʊr/ sustain.

> *Run my hand down the seam just to feel how it's sewn —*
> *If the stitch ain't blind then I'm leavin' it alone.*
> Condition → result · **designer**: "blind stitch" is real tailoring vocab — betrays the eye; the flex is the rejection, not a receipt · perfect *sewn / alone*, open /oʊn/.

> *That's deadstock, can't find it, don't ask what it ran —*
> *Archive on deck, that's a different demand.*
> Statement → reframe · **designer**: "deadstock / archive" name the connoisseur's world; "don't ask what it ran" refuses the price; the "different ___" reframe · additive *ran / demand* (+/d/).

> *Slide on a bomber, you wearing a dress —*
> *I think the mirror is feelin' impressed.*
> Contrast → image · **designer / witty**: his casual out-dresses your formal (judgment, the eye); the mirror "impressed" = it looks so right the reflection approves · perfect *dress / impressed*.

> *They watched me take a loss, they thought it would break me —*
> *The crack let the light in, that's what it gave me.*
> Action → turn · **earned + blessed**: the loss reframed as the source of strength (light = grace); lands on the gift, not the wound (momentum over melancholy) · feminine slant *break me / gave me*.

> *'Member countin' change in the cold for the bus —*
> *The cold couldn't reach me, I never got stuck.*
> Then → now · **earned**: poverty-cold → invulnerability; "never got stuck" lands on momentum, not the ache · family *bus / stuck* (/ʌ/), stop landing.

> *Thought the bag would feel like I finished the book —*
> *It was just chapter one, everything that it took.*
> Turn / realization · **earned**: money reframed as the *beginning*, not the arrival; lands forward, not on the cost · perfect *book / took*, stop landing.

> *Know I be dolo, I don't need a set —*
> *Still spikin' on em', I'm over the net.*
> Statement → image · **peerless / alone**: *dolo* (solo) = no crew needed; the volleyball image shows the dominance instead of stating it · perfect *set / net*, stop landing.

> *Don't move with the crowd, never learned how to follow —*
> *One of one in the mold, they study the model.*
> Statement → image · **peerless**: one of one, originator — they study the model · assonance slant *follow / model* (/ɒ/ + l, feminine).

> *They all wanna claim me, say I'm not like the rest —*
> *I can't even claim me, God made me this blessed.*
> Observation → reframe · **adored + blessed**: the divine-source fallback — the adoration is routed to God ("not mine to claim, it's given") · perfect *rest / blessed*, stop landing.

> *Drop one line, now it's somebody's caption —*
> *I don't even promote, that's just how it happen'.*
> Claim → consequence · **adored / effortless**: a throwaway becomes their identity; "don't even promote" = nonchalance · feminine slant *caption / happen'* (/æ/ + nasal).

> *They walk past you cold and they warm up to me —*
> *Wasn't nothin' you did, it's just somethin' they see.*
> Two-facet contrast · **adored + peerless**: the crowd reads supremacy on sight ("they love me — that's a given") · perfect *me / see*.

> *She want all of forever, I left it on read —*
> *She think we together, but I'm with the bread.*
> Action → reframe · **cold in love**: the want runs one way; "with the bread" shows where his attention sits — not her · perfect *read / bread*, stop landing.

> *She said I never spend time, do you know what the cost is —*
> *Slowin' me down, better cover the losses.*
> Exchange → rebuttal · **cold in love**: her complaint met with a cost/loss frame (warmth is taxed, on his terms) · slant *cost is / losses* (/ɒs/, feminine).

> *I love how I look, she 'mirin the drip —*
> *Hear her talkin' bout somethin', but I'm checkin' the fit.*
> Scene → image · **cold in love + self-supremacy**: self-regard is the answer; he tunes her out to check his own fit · slant *drip / fit* (/ɪ/), stop landing.

> *God on my side, know I can't fear no op —*
> *My purpose divine, don't care about a bob.*
> Claim → reframe · **unbothered + blessed**: the divine source as the floor under fearlessness; "purpose divine" in the stated register · slant *op / bob* (/ɒ/), stop landing.

> *Pray on my downfall, it don't move me an inch —*
> *I stay takin' yards, I stay with the stick.*
> Concession → rebuttal · **unbothered**: acknowledges the sunken place (they pray on the downfall) but lands on momentum — the football image carries the forward motion · slant *inch / stick* (/ɪ/ + stop).

> *Didn't study the craft, it was set in me first —*
> *I was good 'fore I tried, call it blessed, call it cursed.*
> Statement → reframe · **blessed (stated)**: the gift named; owning its weight without hype · perfect *first / cursed* (/ɜrst/), stop landing.

> *This came easy to me, never sweated the rise —*
> *When the talent's a given, you don't question the size.*
> Claim → reframe · **blessed (implied)**: the certainty that comes from knowing it's given · perfect *rise / size* (/aɪz/).

> *They ask how I do it, he made me to be it —*
> *He gave me the blueprint, I just had to read it.*
> Question → answer · **blessed**: the come-up folded into the gift — "he made me to be it," and the grind ("just had to read it") is itself part of what was given · slant mosaic *be it / read it* (/i:/ + t, feminine).

> *Top down, winter, I ain't reach for a jacket —*
> *I reach for the stars, I was blessed with this talent.*
> Image → reframe · **effortless + blessed**: warmth-in-winter, ambition over comfort; lands on the gift · slant *jacket / talent* (/æ/ + t, feminine).

> *I worked while they slept, so the gap ain't a mystery —*
> *I'm not lucky, I'm chosen, the chosen make history.*
> Boast → reframe · **the native sentence**, pure: [state flat: I worked] → [land it as settled fact: chosen]; the grind traces to the gift (not luck — *chosen*) · perfect *mystery / history*, feminine landing.

## How this doc sits in the framework

This is the **top of the stack**. The craft docs are general — they hold for any writer in this notation. This doc is particular: it records where *this* writer sets the dials those docs define, and adds the persona, subject, and idiolect they leave open. When filling a spec or judging a combo, the order of operations is: the craft doc says what is *possible*; voiceStyle.md says what is *characteristic*. A line can satisfy every craft doc and still be off-voice — and this doc is how you catch that. Every other reference file points here for "which way to turn the dial"; this file points back to them for "what the dial is."
