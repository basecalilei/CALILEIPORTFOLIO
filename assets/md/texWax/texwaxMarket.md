# Texwax Market

Covers: the content system (the engine — deliberately first) · channels & funnel · launch.

For most brands content is a subsection of marketing. For Texwax it's the growth engine: the founder's cinematography is the company's main unfair advantage, drops exist to generate videos, and organic reach is what the $25 price point can afford. Treat content as the operating system.

---

## 1. The content system — `PARTIAL`

### The asset
Founder personally produces content: strong cinematography, video editing, and sound design, with professional camera, lenses, and lights. This is the reason organic Instagram is a credible primary channel rather than a hope.

### Hero format — `SET` (D9)
- **Cinematic, staged transformation reels**: a vibey, aesthetic scene; the brush/melt method; a before/after reveal of a garment, pair of boots, or accessory.
- Founder's call, on record: rub-on demo reels are boring; the cinematic brush process is the content advantage. Lead with it.
- **Honesty rule attached (non-negotiable):** the method must be *visible* — melting, brushing, heat. The viewer should understand what produced the result before buying. Beautiful and honest are compatible; magic-without-method manufactures disappointment.

### Content ↔ product coupling — `SET` (D7)
- Every ~20-bar drop exists to generate 1–2 videos. The drop calendar **is** the content calendar.
- Winners graduate (reel hits + sellout → anchor run). Restock announcements are themselves content.

### Subject rule (from the wedge)
- **Core content = accessories**: boots above all, then shoes, hats, bags. This is the promise the product keeps.
- **Garment transformations are allowed as occasional spectacle** — they buy reach (more people wear hoodies than obsess over boots) — but always framed honestly ("for your one favorite piece"), never as the default promise. Do not aim the firehose of new buyers at the maintenance wall.

### Funnel-completing content
- The QR tutorial video (from the quick-start card) teaches both application paths and is shot to brand standard.
- At least some content should show the **rub-on result looking good** — it's what most buyers will actually do first. Position it as its own look, not a lesser one.

### To build (OPEN)
- Reel structure template (hook, transformation arc, reveal, CTA), first 10 concepts, posting cadence, account setup, on-screen text/caption conventions (depends on voice, OPEN-7).

## 2. Channels & funnel — `PARTIAL`

- **Primary at launch: Instagram organic** — short-form reels discovered via the reels tab. Runs immediately; costs time, not cash.
- **Paid Instagram ads: gated.** Do not spend until the bundle exists and real unit economics prove an order can absorb CAC (see texwaxCommercial §2). Single-bar-at-$25 is a content model, not a paid-ads model.
- **Funnel:** reel → profile → product page → purchase → mailer with QR → tutorial → (repeat purchase via new drops/colors).
- Undecided: e-commerce platform (Shopify vs alternatives), email/SMS capture for drop announcements (drops + limited stock make a notify-list unusually valuable — build this early), link-in-bio setup, market geography (OPEN-10).
- **Logged hypothesis, unvalidated:** raw-denim / fade-culture communities as a seeding channel — psychologically adjacent (aging-as-achievement), but heritage-skewed; test cheaply, don't build around it.

## 3. Launch — `OPEN`

Skeleton only (sequencing lives in texwaxRoadmap):
- Pre-launch: identity + packaging done, anchors chosen and produced, product page live, quick-start card printed, 5–10 reels banked before going public (never launch a content channel with an empty pipeline).
- Launch: organic-only; anchor colors + first drop; measure which content converts, not just which reaches.
- Post-launch: drop cadence, graduation decisions, notify-list growth, paid ads once gated conditions are met.
